The spectrum stays empty

Tap Start and allow microphone access. If you said no before, open iPhone Settings → Sinescope and turn on Microphone. A call or another app recording at the same time can block the microphone.

Why doesn't a flat sound look flat?

The FFT view gives every hertz the same width, so pink noise slopes down and white noise looks flat. The ⅓-octave view gives every band the same musical width, so pink noise looks flat there, which is the usual way to tune a room.

How accurate is it?

The frequency reading is within about 0.6 Hz for a steady tone (a tenth of an FFT bin). Levels are in dBFS (relative to the microphone's full scale), not calibrated sound pressure, and the iPhone microphone rolls off below about 50 Hz.

How do I zoom?

Pinch the chart. Double-tap or tap Show all to see 20 Hz to 20 kHz again.

Tones are too loud or too quiet

Start with the in-app volume low; high tones and noise at full volume can hurt your ears and speakers.

My purchase isn't showing

Open Settings in Sinescope and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.
