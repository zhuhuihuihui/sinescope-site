Privacy policy · effective 2026-11-12

Privacy

Sinescope does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Microphone

Sinescope uses the microphone only while the analyzer is running. Sound is turned into a spectrum in memory on your iPhone and discarded. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Exports

When you tap Export, Sinescope writes a CSV and a PNG of the current chart and opens the share sheet. The files contain levels per frequency, not audio, and go only where you choose to share them.

Purchases

Sinescope Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Sinescope only learns whether Pro is active on the device.

Children

Sinescope is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact
