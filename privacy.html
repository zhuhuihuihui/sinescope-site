Privacy policy · effective with Sinescope 1.1

Privacy

Sinescope has no account and does not sell personal data. From version 1.1 the free version shows one banner ad, served by Google AdMob, and uses Google Firebase for usage stats and remote settings. Sinescope Pro removes the ad. This page lists what each one receives.

Ads (free version only)

The banner is served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google receives your device's IP address, device model and OS version, and ad interactions (views and taps). From the second launch, iOS asks whether Sinescope may let Google track you across other companies' apps and websites. Only if you allow it does Google also receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalise ads; if you don't, ads are not personalised. In regions that require it, Google's consent form asks first, and you can change that choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Ads are limited to general-audience content. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. No ads are shown to Sinescope Pro users or in the China mainland App Store, and nothing ad-related runs for them.

Usage stats

From version 1.1, Sinescope can send usage events to Google Firebase Analytics, for example that the analyzer was started, how long a session lasted (in ranges), or that the Pro screen was shown and from where. Firebase also receives a Firebase app-instance identifier, device model, OS version, app version and coarse location derived from the IP address. Events never include audio, names or contact details. They are used only to decide what to improve, never for ads. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Share usage stats. Nothing is sent from the China mainland App Store.

Microphone

Sinescope uses the microphone only while the analyzer is running. Sound is turned into a spectrum in memory on your iPhone and discarded. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Exports

When you tap Export, Sinescope writes a CSV and a PNG of the current chart and opens the share sheet. The files contain levels per frequency, not audio, and go only where you choose to share them.

Purchases

Sinescope Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Sinescope only learns whether Pro is active on the device.

Children

Sinescope is rated 4+. It is not directed at children, ads are limited to general-audience content, and it asks for no names, contact details or location.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
